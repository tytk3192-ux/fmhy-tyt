**What We Look For**:
1. * Does it have movies, TV, anime, 1080p, and subtitles? Can the quality be adjusted?
1. * Does it auto-switch through backup sources when first fails?
1. * Does it automatically go to a shows next episode, and play it without user input?
1. * How are the site's UI, player and search? What sort of user settings does it have?
1. * Does it have watch lists? Can lists be synced with login, a code, or 3rd party integration (Simkl/Trakt/MAL/Anilist/etc)?
1. * Any discovery tools? Examples: recs based on watched content, collections, random button, sorting, mood based, etc?
1. * Are redirect ads (like forced new tab/windows) non-existent, slightly irritating, or almost constant?
1. * How is the sites mobile optimization? Does it look good and work well?
1. * Does it have a custom player (built from ground up, or highly customized version of another player) or no custom player?

✔️= 2 points
🟡= 1 point
❌= 0 points

* Keep in mind that requirements for each criteria, as well as new criteria, will sometimes be added or adjusted if we feel it's needed. 

***

⭐ **Star Criteria:**

* Sites in aggregators need to score 18/18 to earn the opportunity to be considered for a star by the community.
* Dedicated-Server + Multi-Server are less competitive sections, so the score requirement for a star is lower (13 & 17).
